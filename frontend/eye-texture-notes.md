# Eye material assets

These are generated illustrative material textures applied to the three-dimensional eye meshes. They are not patient images or clinical reference data. The interactive scene uses actual geometry with physically based lighting and a continuous scroll-driven anatomy journey.

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. The website uses optimized WebP textures in `public/images/`.

- Iris diffuse texture: `iris-albedo-realistic.webp`
- Four-quadrant tissue atlas: `ocular-tissue-atlas.webp`
- Consuming scene: `frontend/src/components/background/eyeScene.ts`

Production uses WebP textures at quality 94: 381 KB for the iris and 485 KB for the atlas, with the original resolution preserved. `eye-initial.webp` is a 42 KB transparent capture of the complete exploded-eye opening pose. It appears immediately during loading and if WebGL is unavailable. The canvas is exposed only after both textures and the final shaders are ready.

The complete exploded eye opens the journey. The iris, cornea, lens, ciliary body, vitreous, retina, choroid, and sclera then move individually into the same central foreground position. Passed structures recede to the lower left; upcoming structures remain behind the upper right. A final scroll interval returns every component to its original exploded-eye pose, which persists behind the remaining page. Position, rotation and scale interpolate on one continuous, reversible native scroll track. Poses are precomputed on resize so scroll frames do not measure layout or refit geometry. Keyboard-accessible markers provide direct navigation. Reduced motion removes the pinned journey and allows manual exploration. Rendering runs on demand and stops when motion settles.

## Final iris prompt

Create a production-quality photorealistic human IRIS DIFFUSE TEXTURE for a physically based 3D eye, square 2048px image. Exact orthographic straight-on view, a perfectly centered circular iris occupying 92% of the canvas diameter, perfectly circular black pupil occupying 31% of the iris diameter. Natural grey-green hazel human iris photographed with extreme macro detail: densely interwoven irregular stromal fibres, radial crypts, authentic small lacunae, subtly scalloped amber collarette around pupil, muted olive-grey outer iris and deep grey limbal ring. Restrained real human colors, finely varied fibers and non-uniform pigmentation, naturally uneven microscopic details. Absolutely no sclera, no eyelids, no skin, no eyelashes, no eye socket. Outside the circular iris is solid black. This is a flat unlit albedo material texture, NOT a lit eyeball or a sphere: no highlights, no reflections, no catchlights, no glints, no directional lighting, no cast shadows, no perspective, no surrounding scene, no labels or text. All optical highlights will be added by a 3D renderer. Aim for a real biological iris photograph, not illustration, not bright sci-fi iris, not uniform mechanical spokes.

## Final tissue atlas prompt

Create a square 2048 by 2048 photorealistic biological tissue ALBEDO TEXTURE ATLAS for a high-end human-eye anatomy 3D renderer. Exactly FOUR equally sized square texture swatches arranged as a perfectly aligned 2 by 2 grid filling the entire image, no margins, no dividing lines, no text. Each square is a flat macrophotograph of the tissue surface, evenly lit, no directional shadows or specular glints, no sphere, no whole eye, no perspective, no illustration. TOP LEFT quadrant: natural ivory scleral collagen tissue, softly fibrous fine interwoven ivory strands with subtle pale pink branching capillaries, believable moist biological microstructure, not white plastic or stone. TOP RIGHT quadrant: dark reddish-brown choroidal tissue with a rich irregular branching microvascular network, deep wine and soft terracotta red mottling, organic soft tissue, no black background. BOTTOM LEFT quadrant: realistic warm subdued rust-orange retinal tissue surface, extremely fine irregular capillaries, delicate translucent organic mottling, thin membrane appearance, not clean uniform orange plastic. BOTTOM RIGHT quadrant: soft muted rose-brown ciliary tissue microtexture, irregular closely spaced fine flesh striations and folds, soft fibrous detail, not mechanical grooves. All are fine irregular biological material textures for wrapping over surfaces; no large anatomical structures or objects, no cuts, no blood pooling, no diagrams or labels, no checkerboard. Photographic natural details, restrained pigment, no artificial vivid colors, no artistic brushstrokes, no synthetic dot noise. Keep the borders between quadrants at exactly half the image.
